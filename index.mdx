ScriptHub is the fastest way to write and deploy Lua scripts for Roblox. Instead of bundling scripts directly inside your game, you host them in the cloud and pull them in at runtime using `loadstring(game:HttpGet(url))()` — meaning you can update your logic, fix bugs, and ship new features to every game that uses your scripts without ever touching Roblox Studio's publish workflow.

## Get Up and Running in Three Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Script on the Dashboard">
    Log in to your ScriptHub account, click **New Script**, give it a name, and write your Lua code in the built-in editor. Hit **Save** when you're ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the loadstring URL">
    Once your script is saved, ScriptHub generates a unique hosted URL in the format `https://scripthub.io/s/{scriptId}`. Copy it from the script's detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste Into Your Roblox Game">
    Open a `LocalScript` or `Script` in Roblox Studio and add the following line, replacing the URL with your own:

    ```lua
    loadstring(game:HttpGet("https://scripthub.io/s/YOUR_SCRIPT_ID"))()
    ```

    Play your game — ScriptHub serves the latest version of your script every time it runs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  ScriptHub is designed for Roblox LocalScript and Script contexts. Ensure HttpService is enabled in your game settings.
</Note>
